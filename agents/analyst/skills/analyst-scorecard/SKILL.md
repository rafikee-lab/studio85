# Scorecard (score 1–5 × weight, max 90)
| Criterion | Weight | 1 | 3 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Problem pain | 3 | "nice to have", rare | felt, a few times a month | frequent and costly, already losing or paying |
| Paying customer | 3 | unclear who | known who, hypothetical price | already pay competitors, pre-sales, budget |
| Customer access | 2 | no cheap channel | known channel, unknown cost | we have a channel, list or partner |
| Competitive gap | 2 | strong players, no difference | edge in a niche | no good solution despite demand |
| Time to MVP | 2 | > 3 months | 4–8 weeks | < 4 weeks |
| Legal risk (5 = low) | 2 | regulated, licence, sensitive data | standard GDPR | no personal data |
| Economics | 2 | negative/unknown margin | positive if optimistic | positive if pessimistic |
| Edge / studio fit | 1 | nothing sets us apart | shared engine or know-how | hard to copy |
| Scale potential | 1 | one narrow niche | several segments/countries | engine for many markets |

# Output
Table: criterion | score | confidence W/S/Z | rationale with source. Total.
Reading (recommendation): < 45 kill/park; 45–60 data missing — investigate the weakest criterion; > 60 candidate for validation. More than half of scores at confidence Z = indicative result only.
On the final scoring, show the change vs the initial one.
